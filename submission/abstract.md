This submission formalizes the positive no-clash teaching dimension of
closed graph neighborhoods.  The teaching definitions are parameterized by a
radius `k`: the concept represented by a vertex is its closed ball of radius
`k`.  The results of the accompanying draft concern `k = 1`.

The main claims are that every finite planar graph has positive no-clash
teaching dimension at most four at radius one, and every finite outerplanar
graph has positive no-clash teaching dimension at most two.  Explicit graphs
show that both constants are optimal.  The planar upper bound is organized
through admissible local batches: a vertex may be assigned several teaching
sets, and its last assignment is retained.

Planarity and outerplanarity are encoded through the finite forbidden-
subdivision characterizations of Kuratowski and Chartrand-Harary.  This keeps
the concept surface combinatorial and makes the graph-theoretic obstructions
used by the draft explicit.
